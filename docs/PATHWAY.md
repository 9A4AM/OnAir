## 2026-10-18 Satellite beacon
New mode satbeacon (family 107, active 108, std 109, worktree dvbs-028). Built for the owner's capture onair_general_11705.250MHz_2Msps (a Ku-band beacon): carrier at 11705.486 MHz drifting with the LNB, a second CW carrier 545.64 kHz above, the main carrier phase modulated by a 48 kHz subcarrier with 4800 bit/s bi-phase data in 2048-bit frames on the IRIG 106 sync word FAF320 with a counter 0-31 in byte 4 (the format DecSAT describes for Intelsat 19's beacon; recognised from its README, no code taken). First attempt read the stream at byte level (600 baud, 256 "bits"): the frames repeated but were meaningless; the bi-phase chips at 9600/s gave clean frames (all 457 of the 224 s recording, no errors). Lessons: look at the tone phase flips AND the chip-level waveform before choosing a bit rate; the SDR clock is a few ppm off, so a bit clock needs a tracking loop, not a fixed rate. Tests: generator golden frames, chain, impairment sweep, live, engine.

## 2026-10-18 TV sat beacon decoder: decoded view
Renamed from Satellite beacon and moved to the TV group. The frames were analysed word by word over all 512 frames of the recording: sync FAF320, byte 3 FE, counter byte 4, last byte = sum8 of the others (512/512), byte 15 a 1.000 Hz seconds counter, byte 122 a copy of the counter, constants (00 00 1E A3 2B), FB fill, ~20 commutated words, ~60 samples; no ASCII, date, orbit or transponder table. Meanings are the maker's, so the Decoded view shows raw, grouped words with a confidence; the satellite is estimated (BSAT-4b 110E, 214 kHz off) from DecSAT's table of published Ku beacons (data ported, notice kept). DecSAT's status file also says the word meanings are open.
