The logo (variant B, secondary: a TV with a satellite dish and "Onair" on the screen) was designed by Cipta Karsa Studio as work for hire. All rights are assigned to the owner, Kristijonas Adomaitis; no attribution is required.

Files here are masters and derived sizes:

- `OnAir-logo-secondary.svg`: the vector original (2000 x 2000).
- `OnAir-logo-secondary-2048.png`: the dark square, 2048 px master. `OnAir-glyph-transparent-2048.png`: the white glyph on transparent.
- `OnAir-macos-1024.png`: the padded, rounded macOS icon. `social-preview-1200x630.png`: Open Graph / GitHub social preview.
- `android/`: adaptive-icon foreground (432 px, glyph inside the safe zone) and the white notification glyph. The adaptive background is a gradient from #3C4042 to #0B0B0B.
- `web/`: favicon.ico, apple-touch / PWA PNGs (180, 192, 512) and plain sizes 16 to 512.

Packaged copies: `packaging/icons/` (OnAir.icns, onair_1024.png, onair.svg) and `packaging/windows/OnAir.ico`.
